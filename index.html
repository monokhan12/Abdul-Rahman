Abdul Rahman

Abdul Rahman is an AI-driven materials engineer, remote consultant to companies in the United States, and entrepreneur based in Lahore, Pakistan. He combines physical metallurgy with machine learning and CALPHAD modeling, works as a research engineer and senior instructor at the Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (PCSIR), and founded Connected 2 Future (C2F), EduCon and Jobs4DAE.

Research in metallurgy and materials informatics

  • Physical and extractive metallurgy: phase transformations, microstructural evolution and sustainable mineral processing.
  • Computational materials science: materials informatics, machine learning and CALPHAD-based modeling for accelerated discovery.
  • Surfaces, interfaces and degradation: nanotribology, multifunctional coatings, corrosion, wear and oxidation.
  • Sustainable materials: direct recycling and regeneration of NMC lithium-ion battery cathodes.

Toolkit: SEM/EDS, XRD, Python and CALPHAD

Python (scikit-learn, pandas), PyTorch, PyCalphad, Pymatgen, MatGL, Bayesian optimization, agentic AI workflows; SEM/EDX, XRD with Rietveld refinement, XRF, optical metallography, hardness and tensile testing, CSWIP 3.1; MATLAB, OriginPro, ImageJ, Thermo-Calc, COMSOL Multiphysics, LAMMPS and Quantum ESPRESSO.

Experience and education

  • Research Engineer & Senior Instructor, Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (PCSIR), Lahore. Upgraded low-grade iron ore from 36% to 65% Fe, ran SEM/EDS, XRD and XRF on 50+ projects, managed ISO 17025 quality systems and built CBT&A curricula with GIZ.
  • Project Engineer, Machine Zone Ltd. Process planning and sintering optimization for a powder metallurgy facility.
  • MSc Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, UET Lahore. Thesis on direct recycling of nickel-rich NMC cathodes.
  • BSc Metallurgical and Materials Engineering, UET Lahore. Thesis on 316L stainless steel weld cladding.
